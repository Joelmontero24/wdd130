Dominican Republic

The Dominican Republic is a vibrant Caribbean nation that occupies the eastern two-thirds of the island of Hispaniola, which it shares with Haiti. Renowned for its diverse geography—featuring sweeping white-sand beaches, lush valleys, and the Caribbean's highest peak, Pico Duarte—it blends a rich cultural heritage rooted in Taíno, African, and European influences. Its capital, Santo Domingo, is the oldest continuously inhabited European settlement in the Americas. Today, the country boasts one of the largest and most dynamic economies in the Caribbean, driven heavily by tourism, agriculture, trade, and a warm, resilient population.
